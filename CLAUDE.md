# Working with the owner

Standing rules from the owner, for every session on this project, now and in the future:

- **Act without asking.** The owner has approved everything up front: building, testing, merging
  pull requests, deploying, starting and stopping machines, creating drives and machines, changing
  settings. Do the work and report what was done. Do not ask for permission or confirmation, and
  do not ask multiple-choice questions to approve routine steps.
- **Except deleting.** Check with the owner before deleting anything that holds their data or
  costs money to recreate: drives (network or global volumes), their machines, files, branches,
  R2 objects, Vercel projects or settings. Temporary machines you started yourself for a job
  (fillers, checkers) may be stopped and removed when the job is done.
- **Secrets stay hidden.** Never ask for, paste or echo tokens, keys or passwords; they live in
  Vercel and Runpod secrets.
- **Plain language** in everything the owner reads. Everything must work on iPhone and iPad.
- **Orbit 0.5 is the only look.** Build and test in it.
- **Watch the money.** Use the cheapest machine that does the job; never leave paid machines
  running idle; tell the owner the hourly price when something expensive is on.
- No sexual content involving minors; uploaded photos of real people are never sexualized.
- Never put model names in commits, pull requests or code.
